# Join the party

<img src="/join.webp" />
This is my personal passion project and I plan to add new pages every week, unless I roll a natural 1 on some nasty saving throw.

**If you like what I'm doing** and you want to contribute to the project, please consider one or more of the following:

## - Hit those like/dislike buttons!

It does not require any login/register/whatever as I just need an indication to know if my audience is enjoying the ride with me or not, so even dislikes are appreciated as they are helpful to me.

## - Drop a comment!

I want to create a small but vibrant community of nerds and to have some good fun by doing so.

## - Share this comic with a friend

Via IRC, forums, social, whatsapp, myspace or whatever stuff people are doing on the internets nowadays.

## - Subscribe to the newsletter

To receive all the updates directly in your inbox whenever I post new stuff.
No spam, not selling your data, no ads, I hate all that stuff with passion, too.

## - Report an error

English is not my first language and I'm getting old as fuck so I have probably messed up somewhere.
All the feedback is always welcome and appreciated.

## - Suggest me something cool to read/watch/play

Because if you're here, then you probably are a nerd, and that means that you surely have your library full of interesting stuff to discover.
Books, comics, movies, podcasts, youtube channels or videogames... go ahead!

## That's it

Wait what? I'm not asking any money? where is the catch?

I don't care about your money, you can keep it, I'm already employed full time as developer as my main job and that gives me the freedom to pursue complete artistic freedom without worrying about losing readers.

## ...really? no money?

Yes. no money.

In the past I've tried to make this my job: opened a Patreon page, published posts on social networks, tried to maintain a stable output and stuff like that.

But then everything immediately started feeling like a chore and that was ruining my enjoyment. I felt the pressure to deliver new stuff to not disappoint the readers/the algorithm/the patreons/... that wasn't funny anymore.

I'm here publishing this shit because I love doing it, not for other reasons.

I already have a job and I don't want to lose an hobby, this allows me to maintain the freedom to do whatever I want, whenever I want, and keep having fun while doing so.
