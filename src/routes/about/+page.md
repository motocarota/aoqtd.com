## About the comic

<img src="about.webp" alt="Another One Quest to Dust" />

Looking for heroic deeds, romance and epic battles against evil? EW!!
Just skip on the next webcomic, then!

This is my current hobby, is free for everyone and I want to keep it this way.

## About the author

<img src="me.webp" alt="Simone Poggi, author of Another One Quest to Dust" />

My name is **Simone Poggi** AKA **motocarota**, and I’m the author of **Another One Quest to Dust**: the story of our stories in the world of tabletop roleplay games.

I'm a **level 20 chaotic evil multiclass** <a href="https://simone-poggi.com" target="_blank">computer programmer</a>/<a href="https://motocarota.art" target="_blank">comic artist</a> who lives in Italy with his wife raising two kids.

I love reading (comics and books), beach volley/volleyball, playing drums, videogames, listening to music, playing some chess matches from time to time, playing TTRPGs with friends, launching new projects, learning new stuff and doing absolutely nothing at all from time to time.
