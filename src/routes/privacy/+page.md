# Privacy Policy

This is a small, non-commercial personal project. No ads, no data selling, no nonsense.

## Who's behind this

Another One Quest to Dust is made by Simone Poggi (aka motocarota). For any privacy-related question or request, get in touch through <a href="https://simone-poggi.com" target="_blank">simone-poggi.com</a>.

## Analytics (GoatCounter)

This site uses <a href="https://www.goatcounter.com" target="_blank">GoatCounter</a>, a privacy-friendly analytics service, to see which pages get read, so I can look at numbers going up and feel good about myself.

- No cookies, no local storage, no fingerprinting, and no personal data is collected: your IP address is not stored.
- It only counts page views, referrers, and coarse browser/screen/country info, in aggregate.
- No individual profiling, no ad targeting, no reselling of data.

## Newsletter

If you subscribe to the newsletter, your email address is stored by the email provider used to send updates, solely to deliver new chapter announcements.

- No spam, no selling or sharing your email with anyone.
- Every email includes an unsubscribe link, so you can leave whenever you want.

## Cookies

None. This site doesn't set any cookies, so there's no banner to accept: you can read the whole comic without clicking anything.

## Your rights

Under GDPR you can request access to, correction of, or deletion of your data at any time. Just reach out via the contact above.
